How the index works

Half the index is checklist completion: how many of the tracked Baty cards are in the vault. The other half is scarcity: every serial-numbered copy earns 100 ÷ its print run (a /50 is worth 2 points, a 1/1 is worth 100), unnumbered cards earn half a point, and autographs count double. Scarcity points level off as they pile up, so the last few points take real hunting. Supply held is the share of every numbered Baty copy on the checklist that sits in the vault.

Tap + to put a card in the vault. "Details" holds grade, price paid and value. A print run marked * follows the set's standard parallel numbering. Checklist compiled October 2026 from set checklists and sales listings; add anything missing on the Add a card tab.
